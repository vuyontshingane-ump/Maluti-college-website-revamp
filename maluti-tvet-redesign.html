Who We Are

More than a college —
a community of growth

Founded in 2002, Maluti TVET College has grown into the North Eastern Free State's premier vocational training institution, serving thousands of students across eight campuses.

Our Story

Forged from six institutions, united by purpose

On 1 September 2002, the MEC for the Free State Department of Education merged six former colleges — Bonamelo, Sefikeng, Itemoheleng, Bethlehem, Kwetlisong and Lere la Tshepe — into one unified institution: Maluti TVET College.

Our logo captures this spirit beautifully: figures holding hands symbolise our rainbow nation's strength in diversity; the upward arrow represents that every student who passes through our doors can touch the sky.

"To provide excellence, innovation and success that inspires entrepreneurship and employability to transform lives."

— Maluti TVET College Vision

2002

Six former colleges and technical schools merged to form Maluti TVET College under the Free State Department of Education.

The college receives full accreditation from Umalusi and several Sector Education and Training Authorities (SETAs).

2009

NCV Curriculum Introduced

Maluti rolls out the National Certificate Vocational programme at NQF levels 2, 3 and 4 across all campuses.

2018

British Council Partnership

International collaboration through the "Bringing the Learning Home" Skills Partnerships Seminar programme.

2024

Digital Transformation

Launch of the new student online portal and e-learning platform serving all eight campuses.

How to Join Us

Your journey starts
with a single step

Applying to Maluti TVET College is simple, free, and fully online. Follow the four steps below and we'll guide you every step of the way.

Application Process

Four simple steps to enrolment

1

Choose Your Programme

Browse our NCV and NATED offerings and select the programme that matches your career goals and academic background.

→
2

Prepare Your Documents

Gather your Grade 9/11/12 results, ID document or birth certificate, proof of address, and school reports.

→
3

Submit Online Application

Complete and submit your application through the official Maluti Coltech portal at maluti.coltech.co.za — free of charge.

→
4

Registration & Orientation

Once accepted, attend registration at your preferred campus and meet your lecturers and fellow students at orientation.

NCV Programme Requirements

  • Grade 9 certificate or equivalent for NCV Level 2
  • Grade 11 for direct entry into NCV Level 3
  • Grade 12 (Matric) for NCV Level 4 lateral entry
  • Certified copy of identity document or birth certificate
  • Most recent school report cards
  • Proof of residential address (recent utility bill)
  • Two passport-size photographs

NATED Programme Requirements

  • Grade 12 (Matric) pass for N1 entry
  • Mathematics or Physical Science for Engineering streams
  • Certified copy of identity document
  • Certified copy of highest qualification
  • Proof of residential address
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • 18 months trade experience required for diploma (N6)
