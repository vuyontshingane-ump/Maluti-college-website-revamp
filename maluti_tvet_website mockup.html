Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When do 2026 applications close?
Applications for 2026 close on 31 March 2026. We recommend applying early as spaces in popular programmes fill quickly. Late applications may be considered subject to availability.
How do I apply for NSFAS funding?
Visit nsfas.org.za to create your account and complete your funding application. Our Financial Aid Office can also assist you in person at any campus. The 2026 deadline is 31 March 2026.
Can I study at any Maluti TVET campus?
Yes, but not all programmes are offered at every campus. Check each campus's programme listing on our Campuses page before applying. Apply to your nearest campus offering your chosen programme.
Do you offer part-time or distance study?
Selected programmes are available on a part-time basis. Contact our admissions office for details on which courses qualify for part-time registration and available timetable options.
What documents do I need to apply?
You need your SA ID or birth certificate, your most recent school report, proof of residence (not older than 3 months), and 2 passport-size photos. N4 applicants need a matric certificate.
How do I access the student portal?
Once registered, you receive your student number and portal credentials by email. Visit the Student Intranet via the link at the top of the page or contact the IT helpdesk at your campus.
